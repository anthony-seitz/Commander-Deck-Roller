Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Summary

Commander Deck Roller doesn't have accounts and doesn't store your data on our servers. The site runs in your browser. Advertising and analytics partners may collect information as described below.

Information stored in your browser

The site uses your browser's local storage to remember your settings (bracket, colors, budget, and so on) and any card list you paste into "My collection." This data stays on your device. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Card data requests

To find cards and prices, your browser sends requests directly to Scryfall's public API. When you load a collection, the card names in your list are sent to Scryfall so it can identify them. Scryfall's handling of those requests is covered by its own privacy policy. Card images are also loaded from Scryfall's servers.

Advertising

This site may show ads from Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Analytics

We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular. It doesn't use cookies or track you across sites.

Affiliate links

Some "Buy" links (for example, to TCGplayer) are affiliate links. If you buy something after clicking one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. The store you visit may use cookies to attribute the sale; its own privacy policy applies there.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page.
